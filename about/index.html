Peiyao Sheng

I am a Researcher at Sentient Labs, working on LLM reasoning, post-training and agent safety. My research spans verifiable reasoning and process supervision, agent security, model fingerprinting, and LLM evaluation.

Before focusing on AI, I worked on the security and accountability of decentralized systems. I co-founded Witness Chain and built its research foundation on a verifiable physical trust stack. That background shapes how I approach models and agents today: verifying their context and behavior instead of taking them on trust.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UIUC, advised by Prof. Pramod Viswanath, and was a visiting researcher at Princeton University. I interned at Chainlink Labs with Prof. Dahlia Malkhi. I received my B.S. from the ACM Honors Class, Zhiyuan College, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
